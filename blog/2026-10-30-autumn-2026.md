## The degree

In January 2025 I started an MSc in Applied Computer Science at FDU Vancouver, and I finished it in August 2026 with a CGPA of 3.56.

My capstone was about LLM serving. Our team of four built a learning-to-rank request scheduler on top of vLLM, and I owned the KV-cache layer underneath it. I wrote up what we measured and what surprised us in [the capstone post](/blog/kv-cache-capstone). A related side project was getting a 27B model to run usefully on a single RTX 3090, which is in [its own post](/blog/27b-on-one-3090).

## Solana MEV infrastructure, 2025

Most of my 2025 engineering time went into Solana MEV infrastructure. The piece I am proudest of is a Jito bundle relay. I first wrote it in Go, then ported it to Rust. It fans bundles out to 8 block-engine regions over more than 4,000 egress IPs, with token-bucket rate limits and cooldowns when a region pushes back.

Most of the work was about throughput and staying inside rate limits, not about the trading itself. The details are in [the relay post](/blog/jito-relay-throughput).

## A Rust trading system on Polymarket

From late 2025 to the summer of 2026 I worked on a Rust trading system for Polymarket's 15-minute BTC up/down markets. I wrote about 80% of it, 720 of the 894 commits. It covers the market-data feed, signals, execution, a risk engine, reconciliation and gasless claims. It traded live on 895 BTC markets.

Building it taught me more about backtests than about markets. I wrote that down in [what a live trading system taught me about backtests](/blog/polymarket-backtest-lessons).

## Robinhood Chain arbitrage

I also built arbitrage tooling for Robinhood Chain, an Arbitrum L2. I built it detection-first: measure the opportunities, then build the execution path. I explain why in [measure before you build](/blog/measure-before-you-build).

## LLM-Digest

Since May 2026 I have been running [LLM-Digest](https://llm.kitkong.xyz), a self-hosted market-intelligence pipeline. It reads 127 sources and has produced more than 4,100 reports so far. The main report is a nine-stage chain where each stage reads its own material and passes its conclusions to the next stage. I describe how that is built in [nine stages, one conclusion](/blog/nine-stages-one-conclusion).

## RootData and CatcherVC

From September 2022 to September 2026 I was on the founding team at RootData, a crypto data platform, as a product manager. Over that time it grew to 428K users and more than 200 API partners. Near the end we open-sourced MCP servers so AI agents could query RootData directly.

I was also an investment partner at CatcherVC, where I reviewed more than 200 seed-stage deals a year. That work trained a habit I still use in engineering: read the evidence before the pitch.
